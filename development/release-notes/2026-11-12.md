# 2026-11-12

## kategoria Founded

kategoria is a library for computing with categories and for deciding claims about them ([decision 0002](../decisions/0002-scope-boundaries-and-dependencies.md), [decision 0003](../decisions/0003-the-first-modules.md)). It stands on morphe.

- **Every claim is answered with its evidence.** A claim holds, with what makes it so; fails, with the place where it fails; or is undecided, with the reason. What a search finds is checked by a function that does not share its code.
- **Categories two ways.** A finite category is its table of composition, taken from a family (ordinals, lattices, monoids and groups, finite sets, the simplex category) or made from others (opposites, products, slices, categories of functors). A category can also be given by generators and equations: the equations are completed into rewriting rules, and when that ends the category is built whole. Two paths found equal come with a derivation by the equations themselves.
- **Functors and what goes with them.** Functors and natural transformations are verified, counted and found. A functor is told whether it is faithful, full or an equivalence. An adjoint is built from universal arrows, or the object that has none is named. A monad's algebras are built.
- **Limits.** Products, equalizers, pullbacks and their duals are found, or the cone that does not factor is named. A category is told whether it has every finite limit and whether it is cartesian closed.
- **Set-valued functors.** Data on a schema: its sections and classes, its elements, the Yoneda lemma, the three ways to move it along a functor (with their universal properties decided), and the classifier of subobjects with its truth values.
- **String diagrams.** Two terms of a monoidal signature are told equal exactly when their diagrams are the same. The laws of a theory are decided in a model, among finite sets, relations or matrices, and a law that fails names the entry.
- **A language.** A script declares these things and states claims about them. Each statement is answered with a verdict and with what there is to draw, and a statement that is not understood is answered with its line, its column and what could come there.

It is held to 190 checks against closed forms, brute force and structures built another way. The simplex category up to [2] is rebuilt from its faces, degeneracies and identities.
