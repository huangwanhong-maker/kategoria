# Release Notes: 20 November 2026

## Resource Types, Their Laws, and the Order of Grants

kategoria can now say what a resource is and check it ([decision 0004](../decisions/0004-resource-types.md)).

- **Declaring a resource type.** A resource type is declared by its components, each with its kinds and a quantity semiring that fixes whether it divides: units, grains, divisible amounts, signed balances, or qualities that do not add up. Conversions are the free processes; disposal is declared kind by kind, so that chores and waste cannot simply be thrown away. Validity covers capacities and distinct items.
- **Every law answered with its scope.** Each verdict says what it establishes:
  - proved by construction for every type built from the primitives;
  - proved for every bundle from the generating processes, as for conservation laws and monotones;
  - decided exhaustively on a finite model, a proof for that model, where a counterexample refutes outright;
  - found by search with a witness that separate code replays, refuted by a monotone, or left undecided.

  A finite model's laws are decided by kategoria's own constructions. Its totalisation is validated as a monoid, its order built as a preorder category, and combination verified as a functor.
- **Allocations as morphisms.** Giving parts of a pool to recipients is checked for validity and conservation, with the conversions it needs.
- **Whether order matters.** On a finite system of grants acting on states, the order of grants is decided. A shared stock commutes; a price that rises after each sale does not, and both outcomes are named. The pairs that commute are returned, ready for a search to evaluate each trace once.
- **Operators that treat renamed things alike.** An operator's equivariance under renaming items or positions is decided as naturality. The swap mutation passes; an operator that singles out one item fails.
- **Checks:** 39, among them 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O, and a cow whose beef and leather cannot go to two recipients.
